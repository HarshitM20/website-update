Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our doors, laminates, orders and visits.

What do you make and sell?

We manufacture wooden and WPVC doors under the Dudor Door brand, and we supply decorative laminates for wardrobes, kitchens, furniture, wall panels and doors.

What is the difference between wooden and WPVC doors?

Wooden doors have a natural, solid look and suit bedrooms, main entrances and living areas. WPVC doors resist water and termites and do not swell or warp, so they suit bathrooms, kitchens, balconies and damp areas.
